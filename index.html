01 — Our Story

Born from a Love
of the Archipelago

"What if all of Indonesia could be at anyone's dining table?"

Indonesia is home to over 300 ethnic groups with unparalleled culinary wealth. Every region holds recipes, stories, and food traditions passed down for centuries. Yet the reality is: not everyone has the access or opportunity to explore this richness firsthand.

Around Indonesia Box bridges the gap between regional culinary heritage and the demands of modern, practical living. We believe food is more than a biological necessity — it is a gateway to understanding the culture, history, and identity of a nation. Every product we make carries more than flavor; it carries a story.

With modern frozen food packaging equipped with educational QR codes, customers don't just enjoy authentic flavors — they learn about the origins of dishes, local wisdom, and unique cultural facts from each region. This is a new way to love Indonesia.

1

The Idea

Deep research into the potential of regional Indonesian culinary frozen foods — mapping dishes, regions, and untapped demand.

2

The Concept

Developing the cultural education concept combined with the gamified Passport Indonesia system to create a unique engagement layer.

3

The Product

Recipe testing for 9 iconic menus from 9 different provinces — ensuring authenticity while meeting frozen food production standards.

4

The Launch

Initial launch of 5 Regional Box series via e-commerce platforms — Box Sumatera and Box Jawa leading the way.

09 — Social Responsibility

Positive Impact for Society,
Culture & Environment

01

Preserving Culinary Heritage

By bringing authentic regional recipes to commercial scale, we help preserve culinary traditions at risk of being forgotten by younger generations. Every product is a living documentation of Nusantara's culinary wealth.

02

Empowering Local MSMEs

Raw materials are sourced from MSMEs and local farmers in each food's region of origin. Our partnership program ensures economic benefits flow back to producing communities.

03

Cultural Education for Youth

Through educational packaging and digital content, we reach millions of young people with engaging cultural information. Target: 100,000 active Passport Indonesia users exploring Nusantara's heritage.

04

Eco-Friendly Packaging

All packaging is made from fully recyclable materials. We are committed to achieving zero-waste packaging by Phase 3 and using 100% organic inks in the printing process.

Aligned with UN SDGs: SDG 2 — Zero Hunger SDG 8 — Decent Work SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities SDG 12 — Responsible Production SDG 17 — Partnerships
